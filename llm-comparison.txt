Comparing my models.py with the one generated by Claude Opus 5.5, mine is definitely more bare-bones with the bare minimum requirements. Claude opted to put in more fields for user profile types, such as attendance rate for students and office location for instructors, which could be helpful down the line. Therefore, my models.py is significantly easier to implement, and Claude's is, I would say, more complete. I also am not well-versed in the documentation practices of Django models, so my docstrings were rather verbose. I enjoy Claude's succinct docstrings that are then aided with both the attributes and the relationships. While I do not necessarily think all of Claude's code is essential, I think the best result would be to have Claude generate an initial models.py and have a human review and change things to best suit the requirements of the application.
